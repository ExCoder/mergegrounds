# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want a technically rigorous community in which people can question evidence,
threat models, and design decisions without attacking one another. Contributors
must be respectful, specific, and honest about uncertainty. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Security disagreements are expected. Critique the claim, test, control, or
failure mode; do not speculate about a contributor's motives or competence.
Never pressure a reporter to publish vulnerability details before a coordinated
fix is available.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, project events, and private project communication. Maintainers may edit,
hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens people or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's
private contact channel. For a product vulnerability, use the separate process in
`SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled with discretion, conflicts of interest will
be recused where practical, and retaliation against a good-faith reporter is
prohibited.
